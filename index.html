// Who we are

Built by hand, by volunteers, on a stretch of canal bank.

Canalside Activity and Environment Centre is run by Scouting volunteers for the benefit of young people and the wider community. In spring 2016, St George's Mill Hill Scout Group took out a lease on a plot of land next to Feniscliffe Allotments — flattening the site, digging drainage and a pond, and building the activity areas you'll find today.

Thousands of young people from across the Borough now use the site every year, giving groups affordable access to the outdoors that many wouldn't otherwise get. We've also worked with Super Slow Way to run free canoeing, archery and creative workshops along the canal.

The King's Award for Voluntary Service, 2024 Awarded to St George's Mill Hill Scout Group for running Canalside for the benefit of the community — the UK's highest honour for a volunteer group.
// Along the towpath

Everything on site, activity by activity.

Instructor-led for groups new to the centre, or self-led once your leaders have completed an induction.

Canoeing & kayaking

Straight onto the canal — the centre's original activity and still its heart.

Archery

Instructor-led target archery, suitable from age eight up.

Axe throwing

A firm favourite with our older youth groups.

Bushcraft

Fire-lighting, shelter building and woodland skills on site.

Catapults & shooting

Air rifle and catapult sessions, run under close supervision.

Pond dipping

Our own dug pond — a favourite with younger visitors.

Mountain biking

On-site trails through the planted grounds.

Environment & growing

Tree planting and growing sessions across our 2,200+ trees and shrubs.

// On the water

A partnership with Super Slow Way

From 2022 to 2024, we worked with Super Slow Way to run free workshops along the Leeds & Liverpool Canal — canoeing, archery, creative sessions and more, open to the wider community as well as our regular groups.
