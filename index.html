What We Do

Every session is an adventure.

Our activities are designed to spark curiosity, build practical bushcraft skills, and foster deep friendships among home-ed peers.

Fire & Campfire Cooking

Learn flint and steel, spark management, campfire safety, and cook tasty snacks over an open flame.

Den & Shelter Building

Master knots, lashings, and tarps to construct watertight woodland hideaways using fallen branches and natural materials.

Tool Work & Woodcraft

Safe, supervised use of Kelly kettles, peelers, bow saws, and whittling knives to craft functional woodland items.

Mud, Bugs & Botany

Pond dipping, mini-beast safaris, tracking, foraging, and appreciating the incredible biodiversity right on our doorstep.

Practical Guide

How to prepare for your woodland day.

A few simple guidelines to ensure your explorer has a warm, safe, and comfortable time in the woods.

1. Dress in Layers

Wear old clothes you don't mind getting muddy. Long trousers and long sleeves are recommended year-round to protect against scratches and ticks.

2. Waterproofs & Wellies

Sturdy walking boots or wellies, plus a reliable waterproof jacket and trousers. Jeans and trainers are discouraged as they stay wet and cold.

3. Snacks & Water

Bring plenty of drinking water in a refillable bottle and a hearty packed lunch/snacks to keep energy high during active outdoor play.

4. Weather Preparedness

In winter: hats, gloves, and thermal layers. In summer: sun cream, sun hat, and lightweight long sleeves. We run in all weather except high winds!

Our home education and holiday sessions cater for children aged 4 to 14 years old. Activities are tiered and self-directed so both younger explorers and older youths are appropriately challenged.

We operate in all weather conditions, including rain and wind, provided it is safe to do so. We have sturdy shelters and tarp setups. We only cancel in extreme weather warnings (such as high gales or severe storms).
