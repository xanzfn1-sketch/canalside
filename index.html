What a session involves

Child-led, weather-whatever, hands properly dirty.

Sessions run to the season rather than a timetable. There's a fire going, there's food being cooked on it, and there's a good chance everyone goes home muddier than they arrived.

Campfire cooking

Food cooked on an open fire, made by the children. Tell us about allergies beforehand and we'll work round them.

Dens and knots

Rope work, lashings and shelters that actually stay up — then usually get taken apart and built better.

Hands in the soil

Digging, planting and finding out what lives under a log. Worms very much included.

Foraging and wildlife

Learning what's growing around the site, what's safe, and what's living in the pond and hedgerows.

Tools and whittling

Real tools, taught properly, one-to-one before anyone uses them on their own.

Nature art

Making things out of what's on the ground — leaf prints, mud paint, stick sculptures, all of it temporary.

Who runs it

A teacher, a forest school leader, and a Scout leader — same person.

Sticks and Worms is run by a primary school teacher who works across year groups, qualified as a forest school leader and a Scout leader for 10 to 14 year olds. Eight years and counting working with children, a lot of it with children with additional needs — which matters, because plenty of home ed families are here for exactly that reason.

Before you come

What to bring, what to wear.

Pack these

  • A packed lunch and a full water bottle — we break for lunch
  • Waterproof coat and trousers, whatever the forecast says
  • Wellies or boots that fasten on properly
  • Warm layers — the canal bank is colder than town
  • A complete change of clothes, including socks
  • Gloves and a hat in the colder months

Worth knowing

  • The site has compost toilets, not flushing ones
  • Children cook during the session — send allergy details in advance
  • Clothes will get muddy, singed at the edges, and possibly stay that way
  • Sessions run in rain; we only stop for genuinely unsafe conditions
  • Free parking on site at Canalside
Absolutely no nuts on site Please check everything in your child's lunch bag before you set off. This one isn't flexible.
