WHAT WE DO

Hands-on woodland adventures

Every session offers a mix of guided skill-building and unrestricted free play in the woods.

Bushcraft & Tools

Using whittling knives, palm drills, and bow saws safely to craft items from greenwood.

Fire Skills & Cooking

Striking sparkers, gathering tinder, and cooking woodland treats over open campfires.

Den Building

Constructing shelters and forts using branches, tarps, ropes, and natural materials.

Navigating slacklines, rope swings, and tree climbs to develop balance and physical confidence.

PREPARATION

What to wear & bring

Being warm and comfortable makes all the difference in the woods!

1. Layer Up

Multiple thin layers are better than one thick coat. Thermal base layers work wonders in winter.

2. Waterproofs

Waterproof jacket and overtrousers are essential, even if rain isn't forecast!

3. Robust Footwear

Wellies with thick socks, or sturdy waterproof hiking boots with good grip.

4. Spare Clothes

Always pack a full change of clothes (including socks!) in a dry plastic bag.

What we provide

We supply all tools, equipment, shelter, safety gear, and campfire snacks.

  • Hand tools & gloves
  • Tarps & shelters
  • Cooking gear
  • Campfire snacks
  • Warm drinks
  • First aid supplies
