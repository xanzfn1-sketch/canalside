OUR ETHOS

Where wild play builds resilience and confidence.

We believe children learn best when given freedom to explore, take managed risks, and connect with nature at their own rhythm.

CIC
Community Interest Company
Rooted in North West nature

  • Child-Led Freedom

    No rigid schedules. Children direct their own learning, choosing between bushcraft tools, hammock lounging, or deep mud creation.

  • Risk & Resourcefulness

    Safely handling real tools and real fires fosters deep self-trust, problem-solving skills, and bodily awareness.

  • Community Connection

    A welcoming space for home-educated families to make lasting friendships and feel truly understood.

WHAT WE DO

Woodland activities that ignite curiosity.

Every session blends structured skill-sharing with open-ended outdoor exploration.

Fire & Outdoor Cooking

Flint & steel techniques, campfire safety, and making woodland treats together.

Den & Shelter Building

Tarp setups, natural timber shelters, rope knots, and teamwork in action.

Green Woodworking

Using whittling knives, billhooks, and hand drills to carve wooden crafts safely.

Slacklines & Hammocks

Developing balance, physical confidence, and quiet spaces to rest under canopy.

PREPARATION

What to wear and bring to sessions.

There is no bad weather, only unprepared clothing!

1. Layer Up

Thermal base layers, warm jumpers, and waterproof outer jackets and trousers in all seasons.

2. Robust Footwear

Wellies or sturdy waterproof hiking boots with thick warm socks.

3. Spare Clothes

A complete change of dry clothing kept in a plastic bag inside their backpack.

4. Drinks & Snacks

A refillable water bottle and healthy snacks (please avoid nut products).

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Our sessions are tailored for children aged 4 to 14. Activities are scalable so younger children can enjoy sensory nature play while older kids learn complex bushcraft skills.

For Home Ed sessions, parents are welcome to stay, socialise, or assist their children. For Holiday Clubs, drop-off arrangements can be confirmed upon booking.

We embrace rainy days! We set up large shelter tarps and get cozy around the campfire. Sessions are only cancelled in extreme unsafe weather conditions such as high winds.

HAF (Holiday Activities and Food) places are fully funded for children receiving benefits-tested free school meals. Contact your local council or check our booking link for details.
